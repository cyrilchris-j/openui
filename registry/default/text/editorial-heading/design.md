# Editorial Heading

Genre: Editorial
Macrostructure: Asymmetric
Density: Airy
Shape: Sharp
Motion: Subtle
Typography: Serif Display
Color: High Contrast

## Typography

Heading: Instrument Serif
Body: Inter
Mono: JetBrains Mono

## Motion

Fast: 120ms
Normal: 320ms
Slow: 600ms

## Rules

- Hierarchy comes from scale, case and one change of voice — never from decoration.
- Keep the heading to two lines of thought: a lead and an emphasis.
- Set the index label in monospace with wide tracking.
- Keep line-height below 1.0 for display sizes.

## Avoid

- Gradient text fills.
- Letter-spaced all-caps display headings.
- Centring display type over a symmetric stack.
