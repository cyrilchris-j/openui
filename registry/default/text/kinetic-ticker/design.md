# Kinetic Ticker

Genre: Industrial
Macrostructure: Stack
Density: Medium
Shape: Sharp
Motion: Mechanical
Typography: Condensed
Color: Duotone

## Motion

Fast: 180ms
Normal: 420ms
Slow: 2200ms

## Rules

- Reserve the width of the longest phrase; the sentence around it must not move.
- One accent colour on the rotating phrase only.
- Rotate phrases of similar length (within ~40%) so the rhythm is stable.
- Three to five phrases; more becomes a slideshow.

## Avoid

- Rotating inside a headline with a fixed line-height that clips.
- Vertical slides that shift the baseline of neighbouring text.
- Rotation that continues while the tab is hidden.
