# Spotlight Follow

Genre: Industrial
Macrostructure: Full Bleed
Density: Dense
Shape: Sharp
Motion: Subtle
Typography: Monospace
Color: Neon On Dark

## Motion

Fast: 120ms
Normal: 200ms
Slow: 320ms

## Rules

- One radial highlight, low alpha, no hard edge.
- The highlight communicates the active region; it is not decoration.
- Focus must recentre the highlight so keyboard users see the same feedback.
- Disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Avoid

- Multi-colour spotlight gradients.
- Highlights bright enough to wash out body text.
- Applying it to every card on the page at once.
