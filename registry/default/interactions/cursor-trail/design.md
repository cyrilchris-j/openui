# Cursor Trail

Genre: Industrial
Macrostructure: Scatter
Density: Dense
Shape: Cut
Motion: Kinetic
Typography: Monospace
Color: Neon On Dark

## Motion

Fast: 90ms
Normal: 160ms
Slow: 280ms

## Rules

- One trail per page, and only on dark or high-contrast surfaces.
- The trail must fade toward the tail, not toward the head.
- Never on coarse pointers or under reduced motion.
- The layer is inert: no pointer events, no layout impact.

## Avoid

- Trails that change size on every dot.
- Coloured confetti variations.
- Trails bright enough to reduce the contrast of text above them.
