# Asymmetric Hero

Genre: Editorial
Macrostructure: Asymmetric
Density: Airy
Shape: Sharp
Motion: Subtle
Typography: Serif Display
Color: High Contrast

## Motion

Fast: 180ms
Normal: 320ms
Slow: 600ms

## Rules

- Enter at the top-right, exit down the left rail.
- One display statement, one supporting paragraph, one action group.
- The index rail carries facts (counts, dates, constraints), not adjectives.
- The statement must read as a claim, not a benefit.

## Avoid

- Centred headlines over a three-column feature grid.
- Background gradients behind the statement.
- More than two calls to action.
