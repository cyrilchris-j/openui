# Asymmetric Split

Genre: Swiss
Macrostructure: Asymmetric
Density: Medium
Shape: Sharp
Motion: None
Typography: Grotesk
Color: High Contrast

## Rules

- Choose a ratio deliberately: 7/5 default, never 6/6.
- Make the split legible with a hairline between the columns.
- Recompose below 1024px; never hide the secondary column.
- The reading order in the DOM is the reading order on mobile.

## Avoid

- Equal columns with no visual hierarchy.
- Sidebars that become accordions on mobile.
- Gaps wider than the content measure.
