# Industrial Dashboard

Genre: Industrial
Macrostructure: Mosaic
Density: Dense
Shape: Sharp
Motion: Subtle
Typography: Monospace
Color: High Contrast

## Motion

Fast: 120ms
Normal: 200ms
Slow: 320ms

## Rules

- Regions differ by weight, not elevation: hairlines, not shadows.
- Metrics are monospace, tabular, and aligned on a shared baseline.
- Trend glyphs are signs, not judgements; up is not automatically good.
- Every panel states its unit or its scope in the header.

## Avoid

- A grid of equally weighted rounded tiles.
- Sparklines without axes or units.
- Colour as the only indicator of a threshold breach.
