# Marquee Index

Genre: Industrial
Macrostructure: Rail
Density: Compact
Shape: Sharp
Motion: Mechanical
Typography: Monospace
Color: Monochrome

## Motion

Fast: 140ms
Normal: 320ms
Slow: 480ms

## Rules

- Constant velocity; no easing on the loop.
- Pause on hover and on focus-within, always.
- One line of type between two hairlines; nothing else in the band.
- Reduce to static, scrollable content under reduced motion.

## Avoid

- Vertical tickers competing with a headline.
- Gradient fade masks that hide content edges.
- Speeds fast enough to be unreadable.
