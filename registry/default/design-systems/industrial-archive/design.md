## Rules

- Regions differ by weight, not by elevation. Use hairlines to divide.
- Numbers are monospace and tabular; every figure must align in a column.
- Shapes are cut, not rounded. One corner gesture per surface, on the outer edge only.
- Density is a feature: pack information, then use whitespace to separate regions, not items.
- A single amber signal marks attention. Never use it for decoration or branding.
- Motion is mechanical: short, linear-ish, and identical across similar interactions.

## Avoid

- Rounded card grids with drop shadows.
- More than one attention colour on a screen.
- Centred headline stacks with feature columns beneath.
- Soft easing curves that make instrumentation feel bouncy.
- Hiding data behind hover-only affordances.
